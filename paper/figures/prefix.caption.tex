A prefix is a gate (\thmref{Theorem}{VI.5}). One query $q\in\R^4$ with $\lVert q\rVert=2.4$ attends to six content tokens $C$ and four prefix tokens $P$, all seeded synthetic data, with logits $\langle q,k\rangle/2$ and the prefix keys scaled by $1.2$. (a)~Prefix tuning \citep{li2021prefix}, one softmax over $P\uplus C$. The prefix (ochre) takes the mass $\lambda_q=Z_q(P)/(Z_q(P)+Z_q(C))=0.44$, and each content weight (teal) is its value without the prefix (dashed outline) times $1-\lambda_q=0.56$. The strip below plots the ratio after/before for every content token; it is the same for all six to within $10^{-15}$, so the content weights keep their ratios and their order (part~(c), due to \citet{petrov2023prompting}). (b)~LLaMA-Adapter \citep{zhang2023llama} normalises $P$ and $C$ separately and adds the prefix branch with the gate $g=0.5$: the prefix weights sum to $g$, and the content weights equal the no-prefix ones exactly, ratio $1$ (part~(d)). (c)~The value vectors projected on their two leading principal axes, which keep 88\% of their variance, with the hulls of the content values (dashed) and of the prefix values (ochre). The prefix-tuning output (black dot) lies on the segment from $\mathrm{Attn}_q(C)$ to $\mathrm{Attn}_q(P)\in\mathrm{conv}\,V_P$, the fraction $\lambda_q$ of the way (parts~(b) and~(c); the identity~(b) is due to \citet{he2021unified}), and a larger $\lambda_q$ moves it further towards the prefix hull. The LLaMA-Adapter output $\mathrm{Attn}_q(C)+g\,\mathrm{Attn}_q(P)$ (open grey) is an additive step that leaves the segment, at distance $0.65$ in $\R^4$. The script evaluates the joint softmax directly and recovers the identity of part~(b) to $4\times10^{-16}$. The rigidity holds within one layer, relative to the same layer without the prefix: prefixes at lower layers change the queries, keys and values of higher ones.
