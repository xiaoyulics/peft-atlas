Noether hyperbolas and the crossover (\thmref{Theorem}{III.5}, \thmref{Corollary}{III.6}, \thmref{Theorem}{III.9}). (a)~Scalar LoRA, $w=w_0+s\,ba$ with $s=1$, loss $\tfrac12(w-w^\star)^2$, $w^\star-w_0=1$ and rates $\eta_A=1$, $\eta_B=2$. Top: six gradient-flow trajectories (teal; RK4 with step $10^{-3}$) run from the filled dots along their own charge hyperbolas $b^2/\eta_B-a^2/\eta_A=\Phi_0$ (dashed grey; the slice $\Phi=0$ dashed green) to the fibre $s\,ab=w^\star-w_0$ (ochre). The charge is integrated, not imposed, and stays at $\Phi_0$ to within $6\times10^{-11}$ on every run (\thmref{Theorem}{III.5}). From the zero-$B$ start $(1.2,0)$, where $\Phi_0=-1.44$, Adam with $\eta=0.1$ (red) leaves its hyperbola and lands at another point of the fibre, with $\Phi=-0.53$. Bottom: the drift $\max_{t\le10}\lvert\Phi(t)-\Phi_0\rvert$ from the same start against the step size $\eta$ (learning rates $\eta\eta_A$, $\eta\eta_B$). A GD step multiplies the charge by exactly $1-\eta^2\eta_A\eta_B\,s^2(w_k-w^\star)^2$, a change of order $\eta^2$, so over a fixed time the GD drift is proportional to $\eta$ ($1.2\times10^{-3}$ at $\eta=0.0025$, $0.14$ at $\eta=0.2$) and vanishes in the flow limit, where it is $5.8\times10^{-13}$. An Adam step changes the charge at first order in $\eta$, and its drift stays between $0.55$ and $1.5$ at every step size: Adam lies outside \thmref{Theorem}{III.5} (\thmref{Corollary}{III.6}). (b)~Matrix LoRA with $m=n=6$, $r=2$, loss $\tfrac12\lVert W-W^\star\rVert_F^2$ for a seeded target update with singular values $2.0$ and $0.3$, $s=1$, $\eta_A=\eta_B=1$, $B_0=0$ and $A_0A_0^\top=I$ exactly, so that $\Phi=-\kappa I$ with $\kappa=1$, $\mu=s\sqrt{\eta_A\eta_B}=1$ and crossover $\sigma^*=\mu\lvert\kappa\rvert/2=0.5$ (dashed ochre). Top: the closed-form gains $g_\kappa(\sigma^2/\mu^2)$ (teal, the $B$-update) and $f_\kappa(\sigma^2/\mu^2)$ (purple, the $A$-update) of \thmref{Theorem}{III.9}, with the plateau $\lvert\kappa\rvert$ and the balanced asymptote $\sigma/\mu$ dotted. The hollow circles are $u_i^\top(BB^\top/\eta_B)u_i$ and $v_i^\top(A^\top A/\eta_A)v_i$ read off the factors along the integrated flow (implicit midpoint rule) for both singular directions; they lie on the curves, the charge stays at $-\kappa I$ to $2\times10^{-14}$, and the closed-form $\dot W$ matches $s(\dot BA+B\dot A)$ to a relative $1.3\times10^{-13}$. Shading marks the one-sided regime (one gain more than ten times the other, $\sigma<0.70\,\sigma^*$), the crossover, and the balanced regime (gains within a factor two, $\sigma>2.83\,\sigma^*$, grey). Bottom: the singular values $\sigma_1(t)$ and $\sigma_2(t)$ of $\Delta W$, with time running downward on a log scale. $\sigma_1$ crosses $\sigma^*$ at $t\approx0.49$ and settles at $2.0$ in the balanced regime, where the $A$-update supplies $38\%$ of its growth rate; $\sigma_2$ stays below $\sigma^*$ and settles at $0.3$, where that share is $7\%$, as in LoRA-FA.
