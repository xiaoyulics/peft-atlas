Ancillary files for "A Categorical Atlas of Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning: Cones, Orbits, and Gauges".
catalogue.csv / catalogue.json  every catalogued PEFT method (382) and paper (138), verified metadata, formulas, seven coordinates.
arrows.csv                      every recorded simulation arrow M -> N with its explicit map h and notes on the instance.
obstructions.csv                every recorded obstruction with the failing test (O1-O3).
propositions.json               every numbered result with statement, proof and review status.
framework_checks.py             numerical checks behind the [Num] claims (python3 framework_checks.py; numpy).
