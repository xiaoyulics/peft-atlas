Exposé VII–VIII

The atlas

Fourteen methods read through the three invariants, seven coordinates that place every catalogued method in a periodic table, and five rules that draw the arrows between methods.

Do not attack the hard nut with a hammer. Let the sea rise around it until it opens of its own accord.

after Grothendieck
In one minute
  • Each of fourteen methods gets a card saying what it reaches, how training moves it, what a moved base point does and how it merges, every line linked to its result.
  • Seven coordinates read off the formula place all 382 catalogued methods in a periodic table. Some cells are empty by theorem; for instance, no linear method starts at an apex.
  • \(N\) simulates \(M\) when a pointed smooth map \(h\) gives \(\rho_N\circ h=\rho_M\). Five rules produce every arrow (403 are recorded, each with its \(h\)), and three tests rule arrows out; PiSSA and LoRA, for instance, have no arrow either way.
  • Parameters are not reach. At 131,072 parameters on a \(4096\times4096\) matrix, LoRA\(_{16}\) reaches 130,816 directions and LoHa\(_{8,8}\) at most 122,753.

A method is a pointed map \(\rho:(Q,q_0)\to(\Theta,\theta_0)\). Its image germ says what it can express, its fibres and the optimizer’s metric how it moves, base change what a moved \(\theta_0\) does, and extensions cover adapters and prompts. Exposé VII applies this toolkit to fourteen methods; Exposé VIII turns the answers into coordinates and arrows, mapped in Figure 14.

LoRA

Hu et al., 2021
\[\rho(B,A)=W_0+sBA,\qquad q_0=(0,A_0)\]
Image
The cone \(W_0+\mathcal M_{\le r}\), of dimension \(r(m+n-r)\) for \(r\lt\min(m,n)\). With \(\operatorname{rank}A_0=r\), \(\theta_0\) is an apex with deficit \(r(n-r)\).
Fibre
Gauge \(GL_r\). Gradient flow with per-block rates conserves \(\Phi=B^\top B/\eta_B-AA^\top/\eta_A\), and for isotropic \(\Phi\) is solved in closed form, one-sided well below a scale \(\sigma^*\), balanced well above. On the full-rank locus, with channel-uniform hyperparameters, SGD keeps \(O(r)\) of the gauge, Adam the signed permutations, undamped scaled GD all of it.
Base
\(K\) cycles reach \(W_0+\mathcal M_{\le\min(Kr,m,n)}\). Full-rank pointings form three strata, as a set \(\mathrm{Gr}(r,n)\sqcup\mathrm{Gr}(r,m)\sqcup\mathcal M_r\).

Liu et al., 2024
\[\rho(\mu,B,A)=\mathrm{diag}(\mu)\,N(W_0+sBA)\]
Image

The gauge contains \(GL_r\), and gradient flow through the detached norm conserves \(\Phi\). Any gap to LoRA plus a scale, from the same start, comes from training dynamics.

OFT / BOFT

Qiu et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023
\[W_0R^\top\ \ \text{(OFT)},\qquad \mathrm{diag}(s)\,W_0R^\top\ \ \text{(BOFT)}\]
Image
\(R\) is block Cayley (OFT) or a butterfly product (BOFT). With exact Cayley maps the image is an orbit in the level set of \(K_{\rm out}=WW^\top\), and block OFT fixes \(n/b\) partial Grams too. BOFT keeps \(\lvert\cos\rvert\) between neurons if all \(s_i\ne0\), and the cosines if they share a sign. HF’s default Cayley–Neumann OFT is only approximately orthogonal. Regular base point (BOFT: disjoint factor planes).
Base
Exact block OFT on a fixed partition is idempotent up to closure; iterated BOFT with \(m'\) factors reaches \(\mathrm{diag}\cdot W_0\cdot P\big(\prod SO(b\,2^{m'-1})\big)P^\top\) for a fixed permutation \(P\), and, for injective \(W_0\), \(\mathrm{diag}\cdot W_0\cdot SO(n)\) only at full depth. Re-merging HF’s default with small generators can only shrink singular values.
Merge
M1.

(IA)\(^3\)

Liu et al., 2022
\[\mathrm{diag}(\ell)\,W_0\ \ (W_K,W_V),\qquad W_0\,\mathrm{diag}(\ell)\ \ (W_{\rm down})\]
Image
Linear, the closure of a torus orbit, inside \(\mathrm{HiRA}_1\). Zeros of \(W_0\) stay zero, and row lines (column lines on \(W_{\rm down}\)) stay fixed while all \(\ell_i\ne0\). Covariance \(\mathrm{Mon}\times GL\), or \(GL\times\mathrm{Mon}\) on \(W_{\rm down}\).
Base
Idempotent.
Merge
Fuses into \(W_K\), \(W_V\), \(W_{\rm down}\) always; into \(W_{\rm up}\) under SwiGLU, under ReLU for \(\ell\ge0\), and under GELU only for entries \(0\) or \(1\).

LoHa / LoKr

Hyeon-Woo et al., 2021; Yeh et al., 2023
\[(B_1A_1)\odot(B_2A_2)\ \ \text{(LoHa)},\qquad C\otimes BA\ \ \text{(LoKr)}\]
Image

Adapters

Houlsby et al., 2019

Prefix / prompt tuning

Li and Liang, 2021; Lester et al., 2021
\[\mathrm{Attn}_q(P\uplus C)=(1-\lambda_q)\,\mathrm{Attn}_q(C)+\lambda_q\,\mathrm{Attn}_q(P)\]
Image
Unpointed extensions on the sequence wire, since on a head with \(W_oW_v\ne0\) no prefix is neutral. A prefix is an input-gated parallel adapter on attention, rescaling its layer’s content attention weights but keeping their ratios.
Arrows
In causal decoders with content positions offset by the prompt length (as in HF PEFT), every soft prompt is a deep prefix. LLaMA-Adapter’s zero-initialised gate adds a neutral point.
Merge
Not box-mergeable, since on a softmax head with \(W_o\ne0\) and \(W_q\ne0\) a generic prefix makes the head non-affine, even on one token.

PiSSA / LoftQ / QLoRA

Meng et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023; Dettmers et al., 2023
\[W_{\rm res}+BA,\ \ B_0A_0=[W_0]_r\ \ \text{(PiSSA)}\]\[\kappa\theta_0+sBA,\ \ B_0=0\ \ \text{(QLoRA)}\]
Image
PiSSA is a split start, the cone with vertex \(W_0-[W_0]_r\) and \(\theta_0\) a smooth point. Its image and LoRA’s are incomparable, no exact pointing of LoRA\(_r\) passes rank \(2r\), and \(\Phi_0=0\) at equal rates.
Base
QLoRA is LoRA pointed at \(\kappa\theta_0\), with generically full-rank defect \(e=\kappa\theta_0-\theta_0\). QPiSSA splits, then quantises; one-step LoftQ quantises, then fits \(e\), leaving \(e-[e]_r\) only when \(\alpha/r=1\), as HF ignores the scale.
Merge
Exact merges of generic adapters leave the quantised type; type-preserving ones are lossy (Mq), QA-LoRA excepted.

ReLoRA

Lialin et al., 2023
\[\theta_{k+1}=\theta_k+sB_kA_k,\quad\text{fresh }(B_k,A_k)\text{ each stage}\]
Base
Rebased LoRA. After \(K\) stages it reaches exactly \(W_0+\mathcal M_{\le\min(Kr,m,n)}\), so all of \(\Theta\) at \(K^*=\lceil\min(m,n)/r\rceil\). It is \(\mathrm{LoRA}_r^{\oplus K}\) unrolled in time.
Fibre
Per stage, an apex with gauge \(GL_r\).
Merge
Each stage merges (M1).

GaLore

Zhao et al., 2024
\[W_{t+1}=W_t-\eta\,P_t\,\mathrm{opt}\big(P_t^\top G_t\big)\]
Fibre
A backward lens with compression \(P_t^\top\) and anchor \(P_t\), refreshed from a gradient SVD every \(T\) steps. Under Adam without weight decay or full-gradient clipping, each period is exactly one-sided LoRA \(W_k+P_kC\), with optimizer state carried over, not reset or transported.
Base
The anchor is fixed within a period, so integrability is trivial; as a set over its possible sequences of projectors, the moving shape reaches all of \(\Theta\).
Merge
Not applicable, since it writes \(W\) itself.

Each axis has a procedure that uses only \(\rho\), \(q_0\) and the box.

  • Kind. Check whether the trained model is \(f(\rho(q),-)\) with the original architecture (R), whether new boxes are inserted and some parameter value makes them the identity (E) or none does (E°), whether only the gradient path or the schedule changes (B), whether the inputs are trained checkpoints (P), and whether the adapter depends on a task, an input or a tenant (F\(_T\)). See Def I.2, Def IV.1 and Def VI.1.
  • Shape. Read the degree of \(\rho-\theta_0\) in \(q\). An affine map gives Lin and a multihomogeneous non-linear one gives Cone. A group acting on \(\theta_0\) gives Orb, a torus acting on another method’s image gives Sat (Prop II.8), and an intersection gives Meet (Thm II.7).
  • Base point. Evaluate \(\rho(q_0)\) and compute \(S_1=d\rho_{q_0}(T_{q_0}Q)\), then compare \(\dim S_1\) with the generic rank \(d(M)\) (Def I.5). For LoRA this is Thm II.2(b).
  • Gauge. Insert \(gg^{-1}\) on each internal wire between trainable boxes and slide diagonals through copy spiders, then confirm \(\lvert M\rvert-d(M)\) by a Jacobian rank at a random point (Prop II.12(a)).
  • Covariance. Test the identity of Def II.14 on generators of \(GL_m\times GL_n\), in distribution when frames are random or come from data.
  • Merge. Run the rewrite rules R1–R7 of Thm VI.3 with their side conditions, adding the type condition of Prop V.2(d) for a quantised base. The rules are sufficient conditions. They certify M1 and M1→, and M∞ records a box-local obstruction. Whether a whole network admits some other merge is left open.
  • Rebasing. Apply Thm V.3(a) to additive shapes and Thm V.3(b) and (c) to actions.
∎

Methods with different shapes or base-point types are never isomorphic, however alike their formulas (Obs I.4(c), Obs I.6).

VIII.B The periodic table

CreditThe colour key refines the additive, selective, reparametrisation-based and hybrid classes of Lialin et al., 2023; the survey of Han et al., 2024 uses a similar split.

Figure 14 splits reparametrisations by how they touch \(W_0\), additively, by a group action, by a Hadamard product or by selecting coordinates. Some of its cells are empty by theorem. An affine \(\rho\) has the same differential everywhere, so no linear method is an apex, and in the catalogue none is. Corollary 2 of Thm V.3 ties idempotent rebasing to shapes that are fixed subspaces, subgroups or submonoids, and of the catalogue’s 92 idempotent methods, 84 are linear shapes and 7 are orbits. The other, WASI, trains on the rank-\(K\) variety \(\mathcal M_{\le K}\) itself, a set that does not move with the base.

VIII.C When one method simulates another

“Generalises” and “is a special case of” get one reading here. \(N\) simulates \(M\) when a pointed smooth map \(h:Q_M\to Q_N\) satisfies \(\rho_N\circ h=\rho_M\) (Def I.2), so \(N\) reaches every weight \(M\) reaches. Five rules produce every arrow in the atlas; three tests rule arrows out.

The rules produce arrows. In each case \(h\) is smooth and pointed, and \(\rho_N\circ h=\rho_M\) is checked by multiplying out. A1 is Obs I.4(a), since \(\rho_M\circ\mathrm{const}_{q_0}=\theta_0\) and \(\mathrm{id}\circ\rho_M=\rho_M\). A2 holds because freezing \(q''\) at \(q_0''\) leaves the rest of the formula unchanged. For A3, take VeRA, where \(W_0+\Lambda_bB\Lambda_dA=W_0+(\Lambda_bB\Lambda_d)A\) and \(h\) sends \(b_0=0\) to LoRA-FA’s base point \(0\). A4 is the universal property of the fibre product (Obs I.4(b), Prop I.8(a)); for a meet such as HRA only the inclusion of images holds (Thm II.7). A5 is the identity below, from Obs I.7.

\[\rho_{M\oplus N}(q,q_0^N)=\theta_0+\delta_M(q)+\delta_N(q_0^N)=\rho_M(q).\]

The tests rule arrows out. An arrow gives \(\mathrm{Im}\,M=\rho_N(h(Q_M))\subseteq\mathrm{Im}\,N\), which is O1. Images are semialgebraic, or definable when softmax enters, so dimension is monotone under inclusion; and every update in \(\mathrm{Im}\,M-\theta_0\) also lies in \(\mathrm{Im}\,N-\theta_0\), so its rank is bounded by \(\rho_{\max}(N)\). Together these give O2. The chain rule at \(q_0\) gives \(S_1(M)\subseteq S_1(N)\) (Obs I.6), the first half of O3. For the strata, the images of \(\mathrm T\)-type and \(\mathrm S\)-type objects are incomparable (Thm II.2(d)). Inside the \(\mathrm T\) strata, \(S_1(\mathrm T_A)=\{XA_0\}\) contains matrices whose column space leaves \(\mathrm{col}\,B_0'\), so it does not lie in \(S_1(\mathrm T_B)=\{B_0'Y\}\), and symmetrically.

Transport. \(T_*\) is an automorphism of the slice with inverse \((T^{-1})_*\) (Prop II.13). It identifies \(M\) and \(T_*M\) as one object in two frames and does not by itself give an arrow between them.

∎
More: which published relations survive

Some become exact. VeRA is LoRA-FA\((A)\) with a constrained factor, and rsLoRA and a linear parallel adapter are LoRA. Others are weaker than they sound. HRA’s arrow to LoRA\(_r\) lands on a degenerate pointing (\(B_0A_0=0\)) outside the strata of Thm II.2, and LoRA is a special case of AdaLoRA only unpointed, their first-order images having dimensions \(mr\) and \(r\) (Lemma II.4).

The literature in time

Figure 16 dates each method by its first arXiv submission. Landmarks come in rough order, with bottleneck adapters in 2019, prefixes and LoRA’s cone in 2021, the torus of (IA)\(^3\) in 2022, base change with QLoRA in 2023, and GaLore’s moving frame in 2024, the year of 113 of the 382 methods.

What a budget buys

Proposition II.12 separates the budget \(\lvert M\rvert\), what a method trains, from the expressive dimension \(d(M)\), the directions it can move the weights in. The gap is gauge waste (Def I.5), per matrix \(r^2\) for LoRA, one for VeRA (numerically), zero for injective linear methods, at least \(2r^2+m+n-1\) for LoHa\(_{r,r}\) (Prop II.12). At 131,072 parameters on a \(4096\times4096\) matrix, LoRA\(_{16}\) reaches 130,816 directions at rank up to 16, LoHa\(_{8,8}\) at most 122,753 at rank up to 64. Rankings at matched budget and at matched \(d(M)\) can differ (Prediction X.10).

The catalogue

CreditEarlier maps made this one possible: the surveys of Lialin et al., 2023, Xu et al., 2023, Han et al., 2024 and Mao et al., 2024, the delta-tuning study of Ding et al., 2023, and the libraries AdapterHub, Adapters and Hugging Face PEFT, whose source settled many details.

Everything above rests on a catalogue of 382 methods and 138 papers, checked against arXiv and, for library claims, Hugging Face PEFT as of 30 September 2026; 82 methods ship in that library. Each method entry gives formula, budget, locus, kind, merge verdict, initialisation, relations, sources, coordinates and a categorical reading, most with arrows and obstructions. Every entry has BibTeX and an address such as #m-lora.

The atlas is meant to grow. A new method enters by answering the seven questions and exhibiting its arrows, each with its map \(h\); a disputed comparison is settled by an explicit \(h\) or one of the three tests.
