The argument on one page, nine checkable results and the conventions used later.

The right generality turns a hard theorem into a remark, and the remark into a design rule.

after Grothendieck

Abstract

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning has hundreds of methods, usually compared by parameter count and benchmark score. This site compares them as maps. A pretrained checkpoint \(\theta_0\) is a base point in weight space \(\Theta\), and a weight-space PEFT method is a pointed smooth map \(\rho:(Q,q_0)\to(\Theta,\theta_0)\) from a small parameter space. These maps form the pointed slice category \(\mathsf{Meth}(\Theta,\theta_0)\), where the frozen model is initial and full fine-tuning terminal, so every method pointed at \(\theta_0\) sits in the interval between them.

Credit\(\mathbf{Para}\) and the lens reading of backpropagation are due to Fong, Spivak and Tuyéras and Cruttwell et al., 2021. The closest earlier unified frame for PEFT is He et al., 2022.

Model referees (see How this atlas was made) checked all 41 numbered results against their proofs, the literature and the Hugging Face PEFT source. They repaired 27, a second review narrowed many of those, and only the narrowed forms appear. Some results are new, such as the HRA apex; many are known and re-derived with credit.

Three invariants

Image germ · what a method can say

Near \(\theta_0\), the weights a method reaches form a linear space, a cone, a group orbit, or a torus saturation or meet of these. Its first-order image \(S_1\), expressive dimension \(d(M)\), min-cut rank bound and base-point type are read off this germ. Zero-init LoRA starts at the apex of the cone \(\theta_0+\mathcal M_{\le r}\), where first steps miss directions it reaches later; a PiSSA-style split starts at a smooth point (Exposés II.A, II.B and II.C).

Fibres and metric · how it learns

Many parameter values give the same weight; for LoRA these fibres are \(GL_r\) gauge orbits. The optimizer's rates push forward to a cometric \(K\) on weight space, so isomorphic methods can train differently. With per-block rates, the compact half of LoRA's gauge acts by isometries and the non-compact half yields charges conserved under gradient flow. Through the backpropagation lens, GaLore without weight decay or full-gradient clipping is one-sided LoRA with a moving frozen factor and carried-over optimizer state (Prop IV.2; Exposés III and IV).

CreditThe GaLore identity is due to Torroba-Hennigen et al., 2025.

Base change · how it travels

Splitting initialisations, quantisation, restarts and merging move the base point. QLoRA is LoRA pointed at the quantised weights \(\kappa\theta_0\), with defect \(e=\kappa\theta_0-\theta_0\), and its image misses \(\theta_0\) whenever \(\operatorname{rank}e\gt r\), the generic case (Prop V.2). Merge-and-restart with a fixed shape reaches the orbit of the monoid one cycle generates; for ReLoRA, every update of rank at most \(Kr\) after \(K\) cycles (Thm V.3; Exposé V).

Adapters, prefixes and ReFT extend the network; seven local rewrite rules with side conditions certify when they merge back (Exposé VI).

Things you can check in your browser

  1. LoRA+ is \(\alpha\) in disguise. For zero-init LoRA under Adam with \(\varepsilon=0\), no weight decay or clipping, one schedule for both groups and the same data order and dropout masks, LoRA+ with ratio \(\lambda\) follows, in exact arithmetic, the same weight trajectory as LoRA with \(\alpha\) times \(\lambda\). Thm III.10 · Cor III.11 · orbit explorer
  2. No initialisation buys rank above \(2r\). No exact pointing of \(\mathrm{LoRA}_r\), zero-init or split, reaches an update of rank above \(2r\). For \(r\lt\min(m,n)\), its full-rank pointings form three strata, classified as a set by \(\mathrm{Gr}(r,n)\sqcup\mathrm{Gr}(r,m)\sqcup\mathcal M_r\). Thm II.1 · Thm II.2 · apex and smooth point
  3. Two apexes. Zero-init LoRA misses \(r(n-r)\) first-order directions. HRA without Gram–Schmidt, at HF PEFT's default paired initialisation, is an apex too when \(W_0\) has full column rank and \(r\) is even with \(2\le r\le n-2\). Thm II.2 · Thm II.7 · apex · Gram lab
  4. Orthogonal methods keep the spectrum. If every merged weight is \(W_0R\) with \(R\) exactly orthogonal (exact-Cayley OFT, HRA, and GOFT and ETHER as in their papers), the neuron Gram matrix \(WW^\top\) and the singular values never change in exact arithmetic, through any number of merges and restarts. HF PEFT's default Cayley–Neumann OFT is only approximately orthogonal. Cor II.6 · Thm V.3 · Gram lab
  5. Rank is a cut, dimension is a volume. At equal budget, with \(2r\le\min(m,n)\), \(r^2\le\min(m,n)\) and \(2r^2\lt m+n-1\), \(\mathrm{LoHa}_{r,r}\) reaches at least \((m+n-1)-2r^2\) fewer dimensions than \(\mathrm{LoRA}_{2r}\), buying rank up to \(r^2\) for \(r\ge3\) and strictly dominated for \(r\le2\). GraLoRA keeps LoRA's dimension and lifts the maximum rank to \(\min(kr,m,n)\), but caps each block at rank \(r/k\); for \(k\ge2\) and \(kr\le\min(m,n)\) the images are incomparable. Prop II.12 · rank is not volume
  6. Kronecker sums cut the other way. With equal square splits of a \(d\times d\) weight, a generic rank-one update has the largest Kronecker rank, \(d\), while the identity has Kronecker rank one, so zero-init \(\mathrm{LoRA}_r\) and unconstrained \(k\)-term Kronecker sums, for \(r,k\lt d\), have incomparable images. Thm II.11 · cut the tensor
  7. A conserved charge. With constant per-block rates, LoRA's charge \(\Phi=B^\top B/\eta_B-AA^\top/\eta_A\) is conserved by every continuous-time update \(\dot q=-\Lambda D\rho^\top\gamma\), gradient or not, including minibatches, global-norm clipping and DoRA's detached norm. Discrete steps make the charge drift, with relative drift of second order in the step size; Adam and momentum are not covered. Thm III.5 · Noether hyperbolas
  8. Adam sees a finite shadow of the gauge. On LoRA's full-rank locus, with channel-uniform hyperparameters, the largest part of \(GL_r\) an update rule respects is \(O(r)\) for SGD, the \(2^rr!\) signed permutations for Adam and AdamW, and all of \(GL_r\) for undamped scaled GD. Thm III.12 · gauge lab
  9. The activation decides the merge. (IA)\(^3\)'s feed-forward scale always folds into \(W_{\rm down}\). It folds into \(W_{\rm up}\) under SwiGLU for every scale and under ReLU for nonnegative ones, while under GELU only entries \(0\) and \(1\) fold. Thm VI.3 · merge game
CreditItem 1 for LoRA+ (Hayou et al., 2024) under Adam is due to Schulman et al., 2025; the general torus and the SGD law are added here. The \(2r\) bound underlies the PiSSA-to-LoRA conversion of Meng et al., 2024. Balancedness goes back to Du, Hu and Lee, 2018 and Arora et al., 2018, and the two-part conservation law is Zhao et al., 2023. The Kronecker rearrangement is Van Loan and Pitsianis, 1993; scaled GD is Tong, Ma and Chi and Zhang and Pilanci, 2024. Liu et al., 2022 note the (IA)\(^3\) merge into \(W_{\rm down}\), and SmoothQuant and AWQ already migrate scales through activations and meet the GELU obstruction.

Notation

Papers that store \(W^\top\) swap input and output; we translate every method into this convention.

SymbolMeaning
\(W\)A linear box \(y=Wx\), \(W\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}\), with \(m=d_{\rm out}\) and \(n=d_{\rm in}\). Row \(i\) is output neuron \(i\).
\(\Theta\)Weight space, a real vector space of adapted and frozen boxes.
\(\theta_0\), \(W_0\)The pretrained point; \(W_0\) for one box.
\(\rho\), \((Q,q_0)\)The method, a pointed smooth map from trainable parameters \(Q\) started at \(q_0\). For LoRA, \(\rho(B,A)=W_0+sBA\) with \(s=\alpha/r\).
\(\mathsf{Meth}\)The pointed slice \(\mathbf{Diff}_*/(\Theta,\theta_0)\). A morphism \(h:M\to N\) is a pointed smooth map with \(\rho_N\circ h=\rho_M\), read “\(N\) simulates \(M\)”.
\(\lvert M\rvert\)The budget, \(\dim Q\).
\(d(M)\)The expressive dimension \(\dim\operatorname{Im}M=\max_q\operatorname{rank}d\rho_q\). The gap \(\lvert M\rvert-d(M)\) is gauge waste.
\(S_1\)The first-order image \(d\rho_{q_0}(T_{q_0}Q)\). The base point is regular if \(\dim S_1=d(M)\), an apex if smaller.
\(K\)The induced cometric \(D\rho\,\Lambda\,D\rho^\top\), with \(\Lambda\succ0\) the learning rates. Gradient flow is \(\dot\theta=-K\nabla_\theta L\).
\(\mathcal M_{\le r}\), \(\mathcal M_r\)Matrices of rank at most \(r\); the smooth stratum of rank exactly \(r\), of dimension \(r(m+n-r)\).
\(G\), \(GL_r\)The weight gradient \(\nabla_WL\); the LoRA gauge, \(g\cdot(B,A)=(Bg^{-1},gA)\).
